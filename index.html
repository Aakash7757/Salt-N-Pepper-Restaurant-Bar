The place

Unfussy by design.

Tucked along Upvan Lake Road, Salt N Pepper has built a steady following as a no-drama neighbourhood spot — the kind of place you land at after work, before a wedding function, or whenever a plate of chilli garlic noodles and a cold beer sound right.

The kitchen runs on familiar North Indian and Chinese favourites rather than trend-chasing, and the bar keeps pace with a full drinks list. Portions are generous, tables don't feel rushed, and the lake across the road makes for an easy walk before or after dinner.
